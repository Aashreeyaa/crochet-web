Made by hand, loved by many

The Cozy Knot is a student-built e-commerce platform made by B.Sc.CSIT students showcasing how a real handmade business can run online — from browsing yarn-crafted products to secure checkout and delivery tracking.

The Cozy Knot is an online crochet store designed to provide customers with an easy and convenient way to browse, select, and purchase handmade crochet products. The system includes product management, shopping cart, checkout, online payment, order tracking, and invoice generation. An admin panel allows the store owner to manage products, categories, users, and orders.
